Classical Music Catalogue logo options

Five simple SVG directions for a CMC brand mark. These are deliberately compact so they can work as app icons, navigation marks, favicons, profile badges, or alongside the full name.

01 Concert Arches Monogram-like CMC structure with classical hall geometry.
02 Open Score Catalogue/book shape with a musical pulse line.
03 Waveform C Backing-track audio signal contained by a large C.
04 Conservatoire Rosette Institutional, award-like mark built from catalogue cards.
05 Catalogue Grid More literal catalogue/list mark with opposing C shapes.
