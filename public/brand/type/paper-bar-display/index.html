Paper Bar Display

A first exploration of display typography for CMC, using the Paper Bar logo texture as a source. These are type treatments rather than a production font file.

A01paper-filled serif
Catalogue

Most literal paper-texture direction. Useful for sparse brand moments, probably too decorative for frequent headings.

A02ink with paper initial
Catalogue

Keeps readability high while letting the tan paper treatment appear as a controlled accent.

B01vertical bar cut
CMC

Directly borrows the logo's vertical paper bar. Promising for short lockups and section labels.

B02staff-line field
Scores

Musical without becoming iconographic. Could work as a hero-only treatment behind large type.

C01restrained brand accent
Classical Music

Closest to a practical site system: normal readable serif plus one textured word or glyph.

Sourcetexture palette

Tan paper, staff ink texture, and a code-native staff field for future typographic rules.
