# Classical Music Catalogue Brand Foundation

## Product Name

The product name is **Classical Music Catalogue**. The short form is **CMC** where space is tight, but the interface should prefer the full name in primary brand moments while the product is still establishing itself.

## Product Idea

Classical Music Catalogue is a specialist sharing and commerce platform for classical music backing tracks. It exists to turn useful home-made accompaniments, cue tracks, reductions, and practice recordings into a searchable catalogue where musicians can buy, upload, request, discuss, and build reputation around tracks that would otherwise sit unused.

The product should feel credible for conservatoire, teaching, rehearsal, and self-study contexts, while still feeling modern enough to support marketplace, profile, and community features.

## Visual Direction

The working direction is **contemporary conservatoire**:

- Clean, bold, and editorial rather than ornamental.
- Classical without pastiche, avoiding literal instruments as the main identity.
- Useful and catalogue-led, with surfaces designed for searching, comparing, buying, and returning to owned music.
- Warm rather than corporate, using ivory, deep green, aged gold, and restrained rose as the first palette family.
- Accessible, responsive, and calm enough for repeated use by musicians, teachers, uploaders, and administrators.

## Logo Direction

The first logo concept is an abstract catalogue page with staff-line geometry and a small gold resonance point. It does not rely on the initials CMC. The mark is intended to suggest:

- A catalogue page or folded score.
- Musical staff lines without copying printed notation.
- A curated marketplace object rather than a generic music app icon.

The mark is implemented as a themeable inline SVG in `components/brand/BrandMark.js` so it can respond to future dark and light themes without asset duplication.

## UI Principles

- The catalogue is the core working surface, so density, filtering, previewing, and return paths matter more than decorative impact.
- Profiles should make purchased music, uploaded music, comments, requests, and reputation easy to find.
- Uploaders should feel rewarded and visible without weakening moderation or security boundaries.
- Home and public pages can be more explanatory, but authenticated areas should remain efficient and task-focused.
- Tokens should stay semantic so future colour schemes can change without rewriting components.
